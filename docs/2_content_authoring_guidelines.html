SECTION 01 Foundations

General Editorial Mission & Tone

OmniView combines visual beauty with synthesized spoken narration and micro-learning. Content authors must adopt an approachable yet authoritative voice suitable for diverse audiences: high-school students, history researchers, competitive exam candidates, and tourists.

Written for the Ear

Text must flow naturally when spoken aloud by Web Speech engines. Avoid dense academic jargon and sentences exceeding 25 words.

Cultural Parity

The Kannada text should not read like a literal word-for-word machine translation. It must possess natural Kannada syntactic flow (ಕರ್ತೃ-ಕರ್ಮ-ಕ್ರಿಯಾಪದ).

Concise Density

Target 60 to 90 words per main description. This yields a 30 to 45-second audio track—ideal for mobile user attention spans.

SECTION 02 Audio Pacing

Text Guidelines & Web Speech (TTS) Rules

Browser text-to-speech engines synthesize words strictly according to punctuation tokens. Improper abbreviations, unspaced hyphens, or unexpanded numerals cause pronunciation hitches or break word-boundary karaoke highlighting.

Element Avoid (Speech Failure) Prefer (TTS Compliant) Reasoning
Historical Dates & Eras 16th C. CE / c. 1540 sixteenth century Common Era / around 1540 Voices misread "c." as "see" or silent letter. Spell out centuries phonetically.
Abbreviations & Titles e.g., i.e., Dr. Roy, approx. for example, that is, Doctor Roy, approximately Prevents premature sentence termination caused by mid-sentence period marks.
Measurements & Units 253m high, 100km/h 253 meters high, 100 kilometers per hour TTS engines read 'm' as the letter "em" instead of meters if attached to numbers.
Sentence Breaks & Breaths Run-on sentence of 40 words with semicolon; and no pause... Clear 15-20 word sentences separated by standard periods (.) and commas (,). Commas insert a 250ms cadence pause; periods insert a 500ms breath in synthesis.
SECTION 03 ಕನ್ನಡ ಭಾಷಾ ನಿಯಮಗಳು

Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) Script & Unicode Standards

Writing for Kannada in digital media requires strict adherence to Unicode standard codepoints (U+0C80 to U+0CFF). Misplaced Zero-Width Non-Joiners (ZWNJ) or ASCII legacy font encodings (like Nudi/Baraha ASCII hacks) will break text search, speech synthesis, and screen readers.

1. Valid Conjuncts (ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರಗಳು)

Always use native Unicode Halant (್ U+0CCD) immediately followed by the subscript consonant.

ಸರಿಯಾದ ರೂಪ: ರಥ + ್ + ಯ = ರಥ್ಯ / ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ
ತಪ್ಪಾದ ರೂಪ: ರಥ್ ಯ (ನಡುವೆ ಅನಗತ್ಯ ಸ್ಪೇಸ್ ಇರಬಾರದು)

Unwanted whitespace between a consonant and its virama decouples the ligature into an isolated glyph.

2. Kannada TTS Punctuation

Avoid traditional Danda (। or ॥) in contemporary web descriptions. Web speech synthesis models interpret Latin periods (.) and commas (,) with significantly higher timing reliability.

ಬಳಸಿ: ಹಂಪಿಯು ಪ್ರಸಿದ್ಧ ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ ತಾಣವಾಗಿದೆ. ಇದು ಯುನೆಸ್ಕೋ ಪಟ್ಟಿಯಲ್ಲಿದೆ.
ತಪ್ಪಿಸಿ: ಹಂಪಿಯು ಪ್ರಸಿದ್ಧ ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ ತಾಣವಾಗಿದೆ॥ ಇದು ಯುನೆಸ್ಕೋ ಪಟ್ಟಿಯಲ್ಲಿದೆ॥
SECTION 04 Visual Engineering

Image Quality, Formats & Aspect Ratios

The showcase hero stage uses a dynamic aspect ratio clamp (16:10 on desktop, 16:9 on tablets, and 4:3 on mobile viewports). Photographers and curators must ensure subject focal points remain within the safe visual center.

1. DIMENSIONS

1600 × 1000 px

Provides crisp presentation on 2K and Retina screens without forcing users on mobile data to download multi-megabyte payloads.

2. FILE WEIGHT

< 150 KB Target

Use WebP (Quality: 82%) or AVIF (Quality: 75%). Never upload raw camera JPEGs (3MB+) or uncompressed PNGs.

3. FOCAL SAFETY

Central 70% Zone

Keep critical architectural landmarks or subject faces in the middle 70% of the frame to avoid clipping during CSS object-cover adjustments.

CRITICAL SUBJECT SAFE ZONE Keep main structures, horizons & monuments here
Bottom 25% gradient overlay reserved for text

Notice: The bottom 25% of the photograph has a dark gradient overlay in the UI to maintain high contrast for titles.

SECTION 05 Accessibility (A11y)

The Accessible Alt-Text Writing Formula

Alt text is not a copy-paste of the title. It is an objective visual description for visually impaired individuals relying on screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver) and for search engine indexers.

Alt-Text Golden Formula:

[Main Subject] + [Physical Characteristics & Materials] + [Perspective / Lighting / Setting]
BAD ALT TEXT:

"Hampi temple image" or "Photo of chariot"

EXCELLENT ALT TEXT:

"The carved granite Stone Chariot shrine at Vittala Temple, illuminated by warm early morning sunlight with temple pillars in the background."

SECTION 06 Micro-Learning

Interactive 3D Flip Card Crafting

Flip cards are designed for retrieval practice. The question on the front must prompt critical thinking, and the answer on the reverse must provide an immediate, memorable insight.

Front Face (Question) Constraints

  • Length Limit: Maximum 120 characters (approx 15-18 words).
  • Avoid generic Yes/No queries: Instead of "Is Hampi old?", ask "Which century witnessed the construction of Hampi's Stone Chariot?"
  • Include an appropriate FontAwesome icon string (e.g. fa-monument, fa-landmark).

Back Face (Answer Insight) Constraints

  • Length Limit: Maximum 200 characters (approx 25-30 words).
  • Start directly with the core fact before adding contextual trivia.
  • Must be fully translated to Kannada (a_kn) with identical factual accuracy.

SECTION 08 Quality Assurance

Do's & Don'ts Matrix

Text & Audio Guidelines
DO: Use active, sensory verbs (e.g. "sculpted from granite" rather than "is made of granite").
DO: Verify pronunciation with browser speech synthesis before publishing.
DON'T: Embed parenthetical URLs or reference links inside speech descriptions.
DON'T: Use automated translation without human editorial review in Kannada.
Photography & Visuals
DO: Upload imagery with landscape orientations matching 16:10 or 16:9 aspect ratios.
DO: Compress through WebP/AVIF tools to stay strictly under the 150 KB budget.
DON'T: Add burned-in watermarks, promotional logos, or text inside the image.
DON'T: Use vertical portraits (9:16) for main hero photography.
